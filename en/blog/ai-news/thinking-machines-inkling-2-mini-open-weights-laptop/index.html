Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights model that runs offline on a laptop

ModelsHUDERA AI team · 2026-11-20

On 20 November 2026 Thinking Machines Lab announced Inkling 2 Mini with open weights that can be downloaded from Hugging Face. The model has 26.4 billion parameters but activates only 3.4 billion of them per token, and its memory footprint is about 20GB. The company says it is built for laptops and desktop machines, and demonstrated it on a MacBook Pro in airplane mode, with no connection at all. It runs through Ollama on Macs with at least 32GB of memory, and is also hosted on the company's Tinker platform for inference and customization. The new technical idea is that the middle layers run three times per token, so the model gets more computational depth than its stored layer count suggests.

In the company's own figures Inkling 2 Mini beats Qwen3.8-27B on agentic coding tests: 48.2% against 42.2% on DeepSWE, and 77.9% against 73.8% on SWE-bench Pro Hard, with a note in the table that evaluation setups vary by model. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores about 20, level with Qwen3.8-27B, which is far from the largest models that score above 60. The company admits its limits: it is not the strongest small model for multi-turn chat or for instructions with many constraints, and the internal looping does not add factual knowledge. The Next Web reports the license as Apache 2.0, but we did not find the license named on the announcement page we read, and no source we read mentions Arabic or lists supported languages.

What does this mean for your business?Local models matter to a company that does not want its data to leave its own machines: contracts, customer files, internal code. Inkling 2 Mini makes that possible on a powerful laptop instead of a server, but it is a model for coding and automated tasks more than for conversation, and its overall level is clearly below the large cloud models. Before relying on it, test it on Arabic text from your own work and confirm the license terms on the model's Hugging Face page.
