Alibaba launches Qwen4-Max at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens

ModelsHUDERA AI team · 2026-11-16

Alibaba's Qwen team made its new flagship model, Qwen4-Max, generally available on 16 November 2026 according to most reports, while one report says it went live a day earlier. It is the first model on the Qwen4 architecture: 2.4 trillion parameters in total, of which about 71 billion are active for each token, with native image reading and a context window of one million tokens. The listed price through the QwenCloud API is $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, against $4 and $20 for Claude Opus 5.5 and $2 and $10 for GPT-6.1 Sol in the published comparisons. The weights are closed, and reports say smaller Qwen4 models will be opened later, with no date given.

A low price does not settle the question. The roughly 62% cut in cost per task that Alibaba promotes was measured on its own Work360 test and against its previous model, not against rivals. Independent measurement puts the model below the leaders: the Artificial Analysis index gives it 43 points, while the leader scores 65. DataCamp's review calls it slow and verbose, meaning it uses more tokens per task, which eats into part of the price gap. The default API endpoint is in Singapore, and no report we read mentions a Middle East region or anything about Arabic quality. We were not able to open Alibaba's official pages, so all of this comes from coverage that read them.

What does this mean for your business?If you have many repetitive tasks such as classification, summarising and drafting replies, the price makes this model worth a trial, but work out the cost of the whole task, not the price per million tokens. Before sending any customer data, confirm where it is processed and that this fits your obligations: the provider is Chinese and the default endpoint is in Singapore. Test Arabic yourself on real text from your work, because there is no published measurement to rely on.
